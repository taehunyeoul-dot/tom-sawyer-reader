CHAPTER 36 (CONCLUSION) — Conclusion

[36.0.0] So endeth this chronicle.
[36.0.1] It being strictly a history of a boy, it must stop here; the story could not go much further without becoming the history of a man.
[36.0.2] When one writes a novel about grown people, he knows exactly where to stop—that is, with a marriage; but when he writes of juveniles, he must stop where he best can.

[36.1.0] Most of the characters that perform in this book still live, and are prosperous and happy.
[36.1.1] Some day it may seem worth while to take up the story of the younger ones again and see what sort of men and women they turned out to be; therefore it will be wisest not to reveal any of that part of their lives at present.
