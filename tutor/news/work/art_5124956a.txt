NASA Sets Spacewalk for Station Maintenance, Live Coverage Planned
NASA · 2026-08-26
https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasa-sets-spacewalk-for-station-maintenance-live-coverage-planned/

[0.0] NASA will provide coverage as two astronauts step outside the International Space Station on Tuesday, Sept. 1, to replace a spacecraft navigational aid and complete several maintenance tasks in support of space station operations.

[1.0] Watch live coverage beginning at 7 a.m. EDT.
[1.1] The spacewalk is expected to start at approximately 8:30 a.m. and last about six and a half hours.
[1.2] NASA’s spacewalk coverage will stream through a variety of platforms.
[1.3] Learn where to watch online:

[2.0] During U.S. spacewalk 99, NASA astronaut Jessica Meir and ESA (European Space Agency) astronaut Sophie Adenot will replace a retroreflector on the forward port of the space station’s Harmony module to support spacecraft navigation during rendezvous and docking operations.
[2.1] After installing the reflector, the crew will work to install jumper cables for the data-relay systems, prepare the Alpha Magnetic Spectrometer’s radiator for future maintenance, and replace a high-definition camera on the station’s truss.

[3.0] Adenot will serve as spacewalk crew member 1 and will wear a suit with red stripes.
[3.1] Meir will serve as crew member 2 and will wear an unmarked suit.

[4.0] This will be Meir’s seventh spacewalk and Adenot’s third.
[4.1] Meir will move into third all-time for total spacewalks among women at NASA, trailing Peggy Whitson (10) and Suni Williams (9).
[4.2] The excursion also marks the 284th spacewalk supporting space station assembly, maintenance, and upgrades.
